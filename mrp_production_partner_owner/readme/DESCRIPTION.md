Manufacturing orders that process another owner's goods. The *Goods Owner* of a manufacturing operation
type (from *Stock Picking Type Partner Owner*) goes on its orders. Such an order reserves the owner's
components first and the company's after, for its own component moves and for the transfers that supply
them (*Pick Components*, *Resupply Subcontractor*). The goods it produces belong to the owner and stay out
of the company's valuation. The company's own components it consumes are charged to the product's expense
account, ready to be invoiced to the owner. An order without an owner is the company's own production and
reserves only the company's stock.

Two reports under *Manufacturing → Reporting* list the company's material consumed for owners and the
goods produced for them.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Full-time roles carry all their pay in the grade salary. Only duties on top of a job, like leading a line, get an allowance.
  • SI base = grade salary + fixed allowance, floored at the regional minimum wage. Employee pays 8% SI + 1.5% HI on a base capped at 50.6M, and 1% UI on a base capped at 74M.
  • Employer pays 17.5% SI + 3% HI + 1% UI, plus the 2% union fee on the SI base.
  • OT hourly rate = (salary + fixed allowance) ÷ 208. Rates are 150% on workdays, 200% on Sundays and 300% on holidays. Bonuses are treated as Art. 104 bonuses and stay outside the OT base unless you switch the toggle.
  • OT pay is PIT-free for hours within the 40 h/month cap. Above the cap, the excess share of OT pay is taxed.
  • A cash meal allowance is PIT-free up to 1.2M. A phone lump-sum is assumed to be within the company's own rule, so it is PIT-free. Travel and attendance pay are taxable. Canteen meals for workers and staff don't appear on the payslip.
  • Union dues are 0.5% of the SI base, capped at 253,000, for members only. PIT uses the 2026 five-bracket scale after the 15.5M personal deduction and 6.2M per dependent.

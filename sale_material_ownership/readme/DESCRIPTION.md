Temporary module for the separation of the garment company from the trading company, its former
parent.

- Materials are owned through posted vendor bills that carry a correct e-invoice, for goods received.
- Confirmed sales orders are grouped into transfer batches, exploded by their BoMs down to materials and
  netted against that balance.
- The trading company invoices the rest, booked to the clearing account through a fiscal position.

The module shows coverage on sales orders and deliveries. It blocks nothing and posts nothing. Uninstall
it when the transfer is done.

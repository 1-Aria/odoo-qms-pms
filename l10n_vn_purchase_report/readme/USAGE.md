On a purchase order, *Print → Đơn đặt hàng (VN)* or *Đơn đặt hàng gọn (VN)*. Cancelled orders do
not offer them.

The compact version shows Odoo's variant grids when the order's *Print Variant Grids* is set (on
by default; the checkbox shows in debug mode only). Odoo's own English prints remain for foreign
suppliers.

On a blanket order, *Print → Hợp đồng nguyên tắc / gia công (VN)*. It is not offered on purchase
templates or cancelled agreements. Material norms are kept in the product's subcontracting bill of
materials; terms such as losses, leftover material, quality and payment go in the agreement's
*Terms*, which print as written.

The garment company's current pay scheme as configuration for `l10n_vn_payroll`, loaded into a
company on request:

- **Workers:** base salary 3,700,000 + productivity pay by level (Entry 800,000 to S4 2,800,000) ×
  the month's attainment, both prorated by paid days.
- **Production support, office and management:** base salary + performance pay, the grade and step
  amount less the base, × the month's score.
- **Daily-paid workers:** a daily wage × paid days.
- **Overtime:** workday, rest day and public holiday, and the five night categories.
- **Entered amounts:** efficiency bonus, one-time bonus, annual bonus, untaken-leave pay.
- **Options, inactive until switched on:** attendance bonus (`ATT`), responsibility allowance
  (`DUTY`), phone allowance (`PHONE`).
- The statutory deductions, employer contributions and totals the engine calculates.
- Policy parameters (region IV, 26 standard days, base salary, probation rate, attainment cap,
  0.8 h daily OT minimum, the 6-month and 30% check thresholds) and the performance-pay check at
  *note*.
- One salary structure, *VN_GARMENT*, with a rule per component.

## Raising a nonconformity from a request

The request's **Nonconformities** button opens the ones already raised from it, and its New
button arrives carrying the request, its equipment, the equipment's product, the request's
subject as a title and its company.

The product matters beyond convenience: the defect and object-part dropdowns on the analysis
are scoped to the catalog profiles that apply to it. Equipment with no product on its record,
or a request with no equipment, therefore offers no codes until the filter is cleared in the
search panel — the same behaviour as an inspection recorded against a picking. Assigning a
product to the equipment, through *Maintenance → Equipment*, is what turns the maintenance side
of the catalogs on.

## The actions a request produced

The **Actions** count on the request includes three kinds: actions raised on the request
itself, actions in the action plan of a nonconformity raised from it, and that nonconformity's
immediate containment action. An action reachable two ways is counted once.

Both buttons need the *Management system / Viewer* group or higher, since their counts read
management-system records.

Raises nonconformities from maintenance requests, bringing the maintenance side into the same
analysis, catalogs and action tracking as quality control.

A **Maintenance Request** field on the nonconformity, a **Nonconformities** count and button on
the request, and a prefill that carries the request, its equipment and the equipment's product
onto the new record. The product is what scopes the defect and object-part catalogs, so a
maintenance analysis offers the same coded vocabulary as an inspection.

The request's **Actions** count, which `maintenance_mgmtsystem_action` provides, is widened here
to include the actions its nonconformities produced.

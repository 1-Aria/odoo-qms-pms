1. A sales pricelist with one rule per product style (*Product*), the price being the fee per piece.
2. Two service products: the processing fee and the supplied material.
3. *Settings → Manufacturing → Processing Invoice*: the two products and the pricelist.
4. *Accounting → Customers → Processing Invoice*: choose the owner and the period (default: last month),
   check the lines and the warnings, then *Create Draft Invoice*.
5. On the draft: add the late attributions listed and anything else, then confirm.
